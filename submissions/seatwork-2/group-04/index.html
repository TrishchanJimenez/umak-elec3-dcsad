Our app

Hey — here’s what Budgie does. Take a read.

Budgie is named after the bird, and it’s built to be refreshingly boring about money. Five tabs, no clutter, and no number pretending to be surer than it is — if it’s a guess, it says so.

Feature modules16Every corner of the app, designed and accounted for.
Screens drawn61Including all the empty, loading and oops moments.
Destinations5Home, Money, Activity, Plan and Insights. That’s it.
Contrast floor7:1Text you can actually read, everywhere.

Getting in, getting started

01

Sign up, sign in, forget your password — it’s all here. Then three quick questions (your name, what you earn, how often) and you’re in. If something goes wrong, we tell you what to do next instead of just saying “invalid”.

Google sign-inPassword resetThree questions

A Home that grows with you

02

Day one gives you your balance, a couple of quick stats and your latest spending. Give it a week and the charts wake up: trends, where the money goes, and whether your budget is coping.

BalanceTrendsBudget health

All your money, three tabs

03

Accounts, Income and Scheduled, all in one place. Set a bill up once and let Budgie post it for you, or keep it manual — either way you can see at a glance what’s running and what’s paused.

AccountsIncomeAuto-post

Every peso, logged once

04

Everything you’ve spent, easy to add and easy to fix. Paying off a debt? Chipping away at a goal? Tick a box and the same entry counts as both — no double logging, no mental maths.

Quick addEditingRecord this as

Budgets, debts and goals

05

All three share one tab, because they’re really the same question: what happens to your money next? Six kinds of debt are covered, and credit cards finally get a home of their own.

BudgetsDebtsGoals

Credit cards, honestly

06

Cycles, statements, purchases, payments — the whole messy picture. And until your bank statement lands, we call the balance an estimate, because that’s exactly what it is.

Billing cyclesStatementsEstimates

Saving up for something

07

Decide once how much you’re saving this month, then split it however you like. Budgie keeps score and gives you a rough date for when each goal lands.

ContributionsProjections

Alerts that wait for you

08

The bell opens a proper inbox. Read it, deal with it, snooze it or let it go — four different things, so nothing important vanishes just because you glanced at it.

InboxSnoozeAcknowledge

Insights worth trusting

09

Forecasts and spending checks in plain language, always showing their workings and how recent the numbers are. No crystal balls, no finance degree required.

ForecastSpending checksReports

Settings, your way

10

Your profile, your categories, your data, your privacy. Change your password, pick your alerts one by one, and read the help pages even with no signal.

CategoriesYour dataOffline help
